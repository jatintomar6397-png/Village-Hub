Program Benefits

Explore the long-term sustainable advantages, grants, and infrastructure support delivered across all Village Hub partner regions.

Infrastructure & Clean Energy

Installation of solar streetlights, RO drinking water plants, waste segregation units, and paved connecting roads for safer commuting.

Educational Grants & Digital Libraries

Provision of scholarships for promising students, setup of computer labs, STEM kits, and public village reading rooms.

Healthcare Camps & Mobile Clinics

Free monthly medical checkups, essential diagnostic kits, eye checkup drives, maternal care guidance, and emergency ambulance access.

Livelihood & Agriculture Support

Skill workshops for youth in digital tools and handicrafts, modern soil testing for farmers, and direct market linkage to increase income.
