Terms & Conditions

Please review our official guidelines, code of conduct, and resource allocation policies for participating in Village Hub programs.

Village Hub is strictly dedicated to rural community welfare and infrastructure development. Registration is open to local Gram Panchayats, village leaders, student volunteers, and recognized non-profit partners. Project selection and resource priorities are determined using objective community needs assessment, population density, and urgency of water/health requirements.

All representatives and enrolled volunteers must conduct themselves with absolute integrity, respecting local village customs, culture, and privacy. Village Hub enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against discrimination, harassment, political campaigning, or personal monetization of community resources.

All developmental grants, solar equipment, drinking water systems, and educational kits are strictly audited. Financial grants are never disbursed as unverified cash transfers to individuals; instead, funds are directly utilized for verified material procurement and vetted contractor installation with full public ledger oversight.

All media, logos, village project reports, and software tools provided on Village Hub are protected property. Re-publishing our village statistics or project reports for commercial gain without explicit written consent is prohibited.

Village Hub management reserves the right to update these terms to align with local regulatory frameworks, weather emergencies, or government directives. Any major revisions will be published here and communicated directly to registered participants via email.
